Privacy policy

TinVin Social stores the email, username, password hash, posts, photos, stories, comments, likes, follows and messages (text and voice) you create, so the service can work.

We do not sell your data. Posts, stories and profiles are visible to other users. Messages are visible only to you and the person you write to. Voice calls are not recorded; they go between the two devices, and a relay server may be used to connect them. Reports and blocks are used for moderation.

Stories disappear from the app after 24 hours. You can delete your own posts, stories and messages at any time.

You can delete your account in Settings. This removes your profile, posts, stories, messages, comments and likes.
